# Put Minisheet online (about 5 minutes, no coding)

You need a free GitHub account. You already have one.
You do not need to install anything.

## Before you start
Unzip this folder on your computer. Inside you will see:
- index.html  (the app)
- README.md   (a short description)
- DEPLOY.md   (this page; you do not need to upload it)

## Step 1. Create the repository (a folder on GitHub)
1. Go to https://github.com/new
2. Repository name: minisheet
3. Choose Public.
4. Leave everything else as it is. Do NOT tick "Add a README".
5. Click Create repository.

## Step 2. Upload the files
1. On the new empty page, click the link "uploading an existing file".
2. Drag index.html and README.md from the unzipped folder into the box.
   (Drag the files, not the zip file.)
3. Wait until both files show as uploaded.
4. Click Commit changes.

## Step 3. Turn the website on
1. In your repository, click Settings (top row of tabs).
2. In the left menu, click Pages.
3. Under "Build and deployment", set Source to "Deploy from a branch".
4. Under Branch, choose main and the folder / (root). Click Save.
5. Wait 1 to 2 minutes, then refresh the Pages screen.
   A green box appears with your link.

Your app will be at:
https://ashishpatelengineering.github.io/minisheet/

## Step 4. Check it works
1. Open the link. You should see a small budget sheet.
2. Click a cell, type a number, press Enter. The totals change.
3. Click Save. A file named "Untitled sheet.json" downloads. That is your backup.

## On your phone
Open the link in your phone browser, then use Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
It then opens like an app.

## Important
- Your sheets are NOT stored on GitHub. They stay in your own browser.
  Use Save to keep a file, and Open to load it again.
- Never upload your saved sheet files to this GitHub repository.
  The repository is public, so anyone could read them.

## If something looks wrong
- A 404 page: wait 2 minutes and refresh. Check Settings, Pages shows the green box.
- The sheet looks empty after a few days: your browser data was cleared.
  Open your saved .json file with the Open button.
